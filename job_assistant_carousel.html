24-Hour Job Filter & "Post Filter" Ka Pura Secret

Post filter ka kya matlab hota hai aur yahan se direct recruiter ka email kaise nikalta hai.

1. Jobs Tab me "Past 24 Hours" Kyun?

Jab koi company LinkedIn par vacancy post karti hai, toh pehle 24 ghante ke andar lagbhag 30 se 50 log apply karte hain. Agar vacancy 1 hafte purani ho gayi, toh 800+ log apply kar chuke hote hain aur HR shuru ke 50 resume dekh kar hiring band kar deta hai.

Steps: Jobs Icon > Type: "L2 Support" > Date Posted Filter > Select "Past 24 hours" > Apply!

2. "Posts Filter" Ka Kya Matlab Hai?

Company me jab HR ko turant emergency hiring karni hoti hai, toh wo job form nahi banata. HR apne LinkedIn timeline par ek normal post likhta hai: "We are hiring L2 Support immediately for Gurgaon location. Drop your CV at hr.rahul@concentrix.com".

Steps: Top Search Bar me likhein "Hiring L2 Support" > Enter karein > Upar buttons aayenge (Jobs, People, Posts) > "Posts" par click karein > Sort by "Latest" karein!
⚡ Iska Fayda (Zero Competition): Aise posts aam tor par kisi job portal pe nahi aate. Aise post me HR ka direct email id aur WhatsApp number likha hota hai. Agar aapne direct wahan CV bhej diya, toh bina kisi middleman ke 2 ghante me interview schedule ho jata hai!

Morning Refresh Hack: Dot Hatane Ka Kya Asar Hota Hai?

Naukri & Foundit ke backend database algorithm ki poori sachhayi.

Aapka Sawal: "Dot hatakar dobara dot lagane se kya sach me rank karega?"

Jawab: Haan, bilkul 100% karega! Naukri.com ya Foundit ka algorithm ye check nahi karta ki aapne 500 naye shabd likhe ya sirf ek dot (.) lagaya. Database sirf ek cheez dekhta hai: "Last Profile Modified Timestamp: [Current Date & Time]".

Recruiter Ka Search Behavior:

Recruiter jab Naukri par search karta hai, toh filter lagata hai: "Active in last 15 days" ya "Active today". Jo candidate pichle 1 mahine se login nahi kiya, uska profile 20ve page par chala jata hai.

Daily 9:15 AM Par Exact Kya Karein:

Naukri app kholein > Profile Summary ya Headline me jayein > Last me ek space ya dot dabayein > Save kar dein. Bas itna karte hi aapka profile "Active Today" filter me Page 1 par aa jata hai!

Resume File Name Ka Dhyan Rakhein: Kabhi bhi file ka naam Resume.pdf ya My_CV.pdf mat rakhein. Hamesha save karein: Sarfraz_Alam_L2_Tech_Support_4Yrs.pdf. System resume ke naam se bhi keywords uthata hai.
